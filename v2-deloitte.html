I spent four years embedded with an enterprise client, working across data engineering, AI tooling, and delivery. It's the closest thing on this site to a traditional PM track record: real users, a live platform, and a team I was accountable to every sprint — rather than a class project or an independent build.

The Problem

The client's reporting was spread across legacy tools and ad hoc requests — over 20,000 people depended on it, but issues took a long time to resolve, and at least one core data pipeline was slow enough to block downstream production decisions. Meanwhile, the org was just starting to figure out where generative AI could actually help day-to-day engineering work, with no established patterns yet for code assistance or documentation.

What I Did

01

Self-serve analytics platform

Analyzed ticket and usage patterns in Power BI to drive process improvements across a reporting platform serving 20,000+ users.

02

Early generative-AI tooling

Led development of AI-powered code copilots and document-summarization tools — some of the firm's earliest production generative-AI work.

03

Pipeline re-engineering

Re-engineered a legacy SQL data pipeline using Six Sigma methods, unblocking downstream production decisions that had been waiting on it.

04

Delivery & Scrum leadership

Ran delivery as Scrum Master for a 10-person cross-functional team — backlog prioritization, stakeholder alignment, sprint health.
