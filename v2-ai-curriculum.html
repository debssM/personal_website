TL;DR

A product research engagement at the University of Michigan's Centre of Innovation: talk to enough stakeholders to understand what an AI curriculum actually needs to cover, then turn that into a roadmap someone could act on.

Role
Product Researcher
Org
UMich Centre of Innovation
Timeline
2024
Type
Academic project

This is a smaller, more contained project than the others on this site — one research-to-roadmap engagement rather than a multi-month build. I'm including it because the underlying skill (synthesizing a large number of conflicting stakeholder opinions into one prioritized, defensible direction) is core product management work, and it's the piece of evidence I have for doing it at real scale.

Approach

I led user research with over 50 stakeholders — instructors, students, and program leads with different, sometimes conflicting views on what an AI curriculum should prioritize. The job wasn't collecting opinions; it was finding the signal underneath them and turning it into a roadmap with a clear point of view, not a list of everyone's requests.

Outcome

The synthesized roadmap earned organization-wide "Best Project" recognition at the Centre of Innovation.
